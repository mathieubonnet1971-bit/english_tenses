{\color{VioletDark}\fontsize{25}{27}\selectfont\bfseries CONDITIONAL PERFECT}

\vspace{0.5mm}\hspace*{6mm}
{\color{Grey}\large The conditional perfect}

\vspace{2.5mm}\hspace*{6mm}
\begin{minipage}{0.91\textwidth}

\begin{violetbox}
\textbf{\color{VioletDark}FORM}\\[1mm]
\centering
{\Large \textbf{SUBJECT + WOULD + HAVE + PAST PARTICIPLE}}\\[1mm]
\small
I would have worked \quad She would have gone \quad They would have finished
\end{violetbox}

\vspace{1.5mm}
\begin{violetbox}
\textbf{\color{VioletDark}WHEN DO WE USE IT?}\\[1mm]
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.4mm}
\item unrealized or hypothetical actions in the past;
\item past results that depended on a condition;
\item regrets and missed possibilities;
\item hypothetical explanations about what could have happened.
\end{itemize}
\end{violetbox}

\vspace{2mm}
\begin{violetbox}
\textbf{\color{VioletDark}EXAMPLES}\\[1mm]
\textbf{If I had known, I would have come.}\\
\textbf{She would have helped us if she had been there.}\\
\textbf{I would have liked to stay longer.}\\
\textbf{They would have finished earlier.}
\end{violetbox}

\vspace{2mm}
\begin{minipage}{0.485\textwidth}
\begin{violetbox}[height=3.15cm]
\textbf{\color{VioletDark}IF + PAST PERFECT}\\[1mm]
A common pattern is:\\[1mm]
\centering
\textbf{IF + HAD + PAST PARTICIPLE}\\
$\downarrow$\\
\textbf{WOULD HAVE + PAST PARTICIPLE}\\[1mm]
\raggedright
\textit{If I had known, I would have come.}
\end{violetbox}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}{0.485\textwidth}
\begin{warnbox}[height=3.15cm]
\textbf{\color{Red}ATTENTION}\\[1mm]
After \textbf{if}, do not normally use \textbf{would have} in this type of conditional.\\[1mm]
\textbf{If I had known...}\\
\textcolor{Grey}{not: \sout{If I would have known...}}
\end{warnbox}
\end{minipage}

\vspace{2mm}
\begin{violetbox}
\textbf{\color{VioletDark}REMEMBER}\\[1mm]
The conditional perfect looks to the \textbf{past}: it describes what would have happened,
but did not happen or is presented as hypothetical.
\end{violetbox}

\vspace{1.5mm}\centering
{\small\color{Grey}\textbf{Common markers:} if, otherwise, otherwise I would have..., could have, might have, by then...}
